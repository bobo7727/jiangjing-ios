JiangJing Privacy Policy

JiangJing Privacy Policy Updated November 3, 2026

Camera and microphone: The camera provides magnification, photos, and video on your device. The microphone is used only when you enable sound. New recordings have no audio track when sound is off.

Photos: We request add-only access to save media you capture. The app does not read your photo library.

On-device media: Capture files and save status remain in private app storage so you can retry after failures, denied access, or interruptions. Closing a result does not delete the file. On-device Media lets you share, retry saving, or explicitly discard copies. Copies remain until discarded or the app is uninstalled. Copies already saved to Photos or shared elsewhere are managed by those apps. Unfinalized movies may not be recoverable.

Sharing: Only when you use the system share sheet is the selected media handed to the app or service you choose. Its subsequent handling is governed by that service’s policy.

The app requires no account, includes no advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs, and does not upload photos, video, audio, or usage records to the developer. Photos, iCloud, and sharing services may sync according to system and service settings. You can change camera, microphone, and Photos permissions in Settings.
